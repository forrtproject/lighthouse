How it works

From a field to the evidence in three steps

  1. 1

    Pick a field

    Start from one of the fields, then choose a discipline inside it.

  2. 2

    Narrow by cluster

    Disciplines are split into clusters of related effects, such as “Social influence” or “Memory”.

  3. 3

    Read the timeline

    Open an effect to see its evidence in date order, from the original study to the latest replication or critique.

Kinds of evidence

Foundational
The original study that reported the effect.
Replication
A new study that tests whether the finding holds with new data.
Critique
A reanalysis or commentary that questions the finding.
Meta-analysis
Results pooled across many studies.
Wikipedia
Public coverage of the effect.

Foundational papers that were later retracted are flagged using the Retraction Watch database.
